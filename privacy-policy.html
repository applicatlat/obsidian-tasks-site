Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Obsidian Google Tasks Integration handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the application with your Google Account, the application may access information necessary to provide Google Tasks functionality.

This may include your Google Tasks lists and tasks, including task titles, task details, due dates, completion status, and other information made available through the Google Tasks API.

2. How We Use Information

Google Tasks information is used solely to provide the functionality requested by the user, including displaying, creating, updating, and completing Google Tasks through the Obsidian environment.

3. Data Storage

The website itself does not provide a server-side database for storing users' Google Tasks content.

Authentication information and API access are handled through the Google OAuth authorization mechanism and the application using the integration.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share users' Google Tasks information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

5. Google API Services

The application's use of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy .

6. User Control

You can revoke the application's access to your Google Account at any time through your Google Account security settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when necessary. Any changes will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the application, please contact the developer through the contact information associated with the application.
